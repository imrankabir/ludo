🎲 LUDO STAR

Classic Multiplayer Dice Game
🏆
⚀
Red player's turn. Roll the dice!
Game Rules:
• Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your yard.
• Roll a 6 to get another turn.
• Capture opponents by landing on their tokens.
• Safe squares protect tokens from capture.
• Exact dice value is required to reach home.
• First player to finish all four tokens wins.
