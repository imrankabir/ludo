Yellow player's turn. Roll the dice!
Rules: A six grants another roll before movement. Three consecutive sixes cancel the sequence and pass the turn. After rolling ends, click a movable token to see its legal pending-die options; choose any listed die, then repeat for remaining dice. Duplicate values are consumed one occurrence at a time, and the same token or different tokens may be selected for different dice. Capturing a lone opponent token grants an extra roll. A stack of two or more opponent tokens on one cell cannot be captured. The current player's available move count and pending dice are shown above. Progress is saved in this browser's local storage.
