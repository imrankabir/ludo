Traditional 4-player board • complete all 4 tokens to win
⚀
Yellow player's turn. Roll the dice!
51 common-route positions 6 home cells / player 4 tokens / player All 4 required to win
Classic rules implemented: Roll 6 to bring a token out of the yard. A token completes the 52-cell common route and then automatically enters its own colored home lane. An exact roll is required to reach the final home cell. Safe/start cells protect tokens. Rolling 6 or capturing gives an extra turn. A player wins only after all four of that player's tokens reach the final home.
